Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

The Privacy Statement that follows describes the collection, use and disclosure of information practices pertaining to StopReg.com (the "Site"), which is owned and operated by NETBOOSTERS LAB (collectively "StopReg", "we", "us" or "our"). By accessing, using or registering on the Site, you accept and agree to this Privacy Statement. StopReg provides services related to email verification and signup fraud prevention services.

1. How We Use Your Data

StopReg processes information submitted through our services to provide email and domain validation, classification, analytics, security, abuse prevention, and account management. Depending on the service being used, StopReg may process information to:

  • Validate email addresses and domains
  • Classify email addresses and domains
  • Identify disposable, relay, private, public, role-based, and alias email sources
  • Identify EDU, ISP, and free subdomain sources
  • Maintain and improve detection data
  • Analyze service usage
  • Provide domain management and enforcement features
  • Protect the security and integrity of our services
  • Provide billing, account management, and customer support

2. Email API

When the Email API is used, the submitted email address may be stored for analytical and operational purposes, including maintaining detection data, monitoring service usage, security, abuse prevention, and improving StopReg's classification systems.

The Email API may analyze characteristics including:

  • Disposable (is_disposable)
  • Relay (is_relay)
  • Private (is_private)
  • Public (is_public)
  • Role-based (is_role_based)
  • Alias (is_alias)
  • Alias method (alias_method)
  • EDU (is_edu)
  • ISP (is_isp)
  • Free subdomain (is_free_subdomain)

The Email API may also perform domain syntax validation, MX record verification, mail provider identification, and mail infrastructure analysis.

3. Domain API

When the Domain API is used, the submitted domain may be stored for analytical and operational purposes, including maintaining domain detection data, monitoring service usage, security, abuse prevention, and improving StopReg's classification systems.

The Domain API does not require the complete email address.

The Domain API may perform:

  • Disposable domain detection
  • Relay domain detection
  • Private and public domain classification
  • EDU domain identification
  • ISP domain identification
  • Free subdomain identification
  • Domain syntax validation
  • MX record verification
  • Mail provider identification
  • Domain infrastructure analysis

4. StopReg Classification

StopReg analyzes submitted email addresses and domains using multiple validation and classification signals. Depending on the endpoint and information available, classification results may include:

  • Disposable (is_disposable)
  • Relay (is_relay)
  • Private (is_private)
  • Public (is_public)
  • Role-based (is_role_based)
  • Alias (is_alias)
  • Alias Method (alias_method)
  • EDU (is_edu)
  • ISP (is_isp)
  • Free Subdomain (is_free_subdomain)

These classifications help customers understand the characteristics of an email address or domain and determine how they want to handle it within their own systems.

5. Domain Management

Domain Management features are available on the Scale plan and higher. Depending on the customer's subscription and configuration, Domain Management may include:

  • Allowlist
  • Blocklist
  • Form Abuse Shield

Allowlist: Customers can add domains or email addresses to an allowlist to explicitly permit matching sources according to their configuration.

Blocklist: Customers can add domains or email addresses to a blocklist to prevent matching sources from being accepted according to their configuration.

Form Abuse Shield: Form Abuse Shield monitors registration activity associated with private and relay domains. When a domain reaches the registration threshold configured by the customer within the selected time window, StopReg can automatically notify the customer or block further registrations from that domain. Depending on the customer's selected configuration, the system can:

  • Notify Mode: Automatically send an email alert containing relevant domain details and threshold information.
  • Block Mode: Automatically block the domain from making further registrations.

To provide Form Abuse Shield, StopReg may process information such as the domain associated with a registration, registration activity, registration counts, configured thresholds, selected time windows, and the action selected by the customer.

6. Enforcement Policy

StopReg's Enforcement Policy allows customers to configure how email addresses and domains are handled based on StopReg classification results. Depending on the customer's configuration, classification results may be used to:

  • Allow an email address or domain
  • Warn or flag an email address or domain for review
  • Block an email address or domain

To provide this feature, StopReg may process classification results, submitted email addresses or domains, and the customer's configured enforcement settings. The detailed functionality and configuration options for Enforcement Policy are provided on the separate Enforcement Policy page. Customers are responsible for configuring and using Enforcement Policy according to their own requirements and applicable laws.

7. Data Retention

StopReg retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, analyze, and improve our services.

Email API: Email Addresses: When the Email API is used, submitted email addresses may be stored for analytical and operational purposes. This may include maintaining detection data, monitoring service usage, preventing abuse, maintaining security, and improving the accuracy and performance of StopReg's classification systems.

Domain API: Domains: When the Domain API is used, submitted domain names may be stored for analytical and operational purposes. This may include maintaining domain detection data, monitoring service usage, preventing abuse, maintaining security, and improving the accuracy and performance of StopReg's classification systems.

Account Information: Account information is retained only as necessary for billing, support, and account management.

Deletion Requests: Requests for deletion of account information or personal data can be submitted at any time by contacting: support@stopreg.com

We may retain certain information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or protect the security and integrity of our services.
