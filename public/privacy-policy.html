Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 16 December, 2025.

Information We Collect

Stopreg.com is built with data minimization at its core. We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide email validation services.


Data We Process
Email addresses submitted for validation (Full Email API)
Email domains submitted for validation (Domain-Only API)
Account information such as name, email, and payment details for account management


Data We Do Not Collect
Any personal data not required for validation
Unnecessary user identifiers

How We Use Your Data

Email addresses and domains submitted to Stopreg.com are used exclusively for validation purposes:


Full Email API: Performs disposable email detection, role-based checks, public provider identification, syntax validation, and MX record verification.
Domain-Only API: Performs disposable email detection, domain syntax validation, and MX record verification without processing the full email.


Important: All email addresses are deleted immediately after validation. No data is stored long-term.

Data Retention

Email addresses: Deleted Immediately after processing
Domains: Deleted Immediately after processing
Account information: Retained only as necessary for billing, support, and account management


Requests for deletion of account information can be submitted at any time by contacting support@stopreg.com

GDPR & Data Protection Compliance

Stopreg.com complies with GDPR by:
Minimizing data collection: Only what is necessary for validation
Providing a domain-only API: For clients who prefer not to transmit full email addresses
Deleting data immediately after use: Ensuring no personal data is retained unnecessarily
Processing data securely: Using encryption, secure API access, and strict access controls

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.


Data may be shared only:
With payment processors to complete transactions
As required by law or to protect legal rights
With trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements

Security Measures

Stopreg.com implements technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data:
Encrypted transmission of API requests
Secure storage for account-related data only
Access control: Only authorized personnel may process data
Regular audits of security practices

Your Privacy Rights

As a user, you have the right to:
Request deletion of your account or personal data
Access information we hold about your account
Request corrections to your account data
Withdraw consent for processing account information


Requests can be submitted to support@stopreg.com

Cookies and Tracking

Stopreg.com uses minimal cookies necessary for website functionality and analytics. Cookies are not used for tracking personal data for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to third-party services. Stopreg.com is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with the effective date. Continued use of Stopreg.com constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
