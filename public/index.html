What Stopreg Checks

Comprehensive email validation in milliseconds

Disposable Email Detection

Detect temporary and throwaway email addresses commonly used to abuse sign-ups and free trials with our auto-updated database.

Role-Based Detection

Identify non-personal addresses such as admin@, support@, info@, and sales@ to ensure real user engagement.

Public Provider Detection

Detect emails from public providers like Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook, and others for better user segmentation.

Syntax Validation

Verify that an email address follows proper formatting rules before processing to prevent errors.

MX Record Verification

Check whether the email domain has valid MX records and can actually receive emails.

Email forwarding / alias services

Detects domains that forward emails (including relay or alias providers often abused for signups).
