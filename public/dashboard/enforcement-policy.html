Color Guide

Shows what each policy action means.

Allow

Accept Without Restrictions

Email address is accepted and user proceeds with signup. No flags or additional verification required.

Accept email addresses without restriction.

Warn

Flag for Review

Email is accepted and signup proceeds, but the address is marked for manual review and verification. Useful for suspicious but not definitively fraudulent addresses.

Allow the email but flag it for review.

Block

Reject & Block Signup

Email address is rejected. User cannot signup with this email and must use a different address. Prevents fraud and abuse at the source.

Reject email addresses and prevent signups.

High Risk

Temporary Email Services

Throwaway email providers (10minutemail, tempmail, etc). High fraud risk. Accounts are typically abandoned quickly.

Temporary email services.

Domain has no valid MX records. Emails cannot be delivered. Likely a typo, fake domain, or expired registration.

No valid MX records

Native Email Alias

Alternate email address at same domain that delivers to same mailbox. Hard to audit who owns the account.

Medium Risk

Email Alias: Forwarding

Forwarding Email Alias

Email forwards to a different address/mailbox. Adds layer of indirection. Harder to track true account owner. Medium risk.

Forwards emails to another mailbox.

Generic Role/Department Email

Generic addresses like 'admin@', 'support@', 'info@'. Not linked to specific person. May indicate automated/bot usage. Medium risk.

Generic role or department email.
